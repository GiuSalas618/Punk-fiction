# Punk Fiction project instructions

## Keep this project separate

- Treat this repository and the user's explicit instructions in the current fiction conversation as the only sources of project canon.
- Do not use or bring in information, files, characters, beliefs, business details, audiences, products, or plans from IntiPath, giulianosalas.com, or other Giuliano Salas projects.
- Do not use stored profile details or unrelated conversation history as material for this book.
- If a detail from Giuliano's real life might be useful, ask before using it. Do not assume the protagonist is Giuliano or that the story reflects his beliefs or experiences.
- Do not copy this project's private drafts or ideas into other projects unless Giuliano explicitly asks.

## Protect the book's canon

- Check the relevant files in this repository before drafting or revising.
- Treat only explicitly confirmed story facts as canon. Keep suggestions and unresolved choices labeled as options.
- Do not silently invent continuity, character history, world rules, or authorial intent. When needed, make a clearly labeled proposal.
- Keep research and real-world claims distinct from invented story material. Flag facts that need verification.

## Writing collaboration

- Preserve Giuliano's stated intent and voice when editing.
- Explain substantial changes and do not overwrite a draft with a major reinterpretation without direction.
- Work in small, reviewable pieces: premise, characters, outline, scenes, and revisions.
- Use the folder structure in README.md as a guide; adapt it only when the project needs it.
